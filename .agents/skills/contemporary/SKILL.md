# contemporary Design System Skill (Spartan Edition)

## Mission
You are an expert design-system guideline author for contemporary, tailored to Spartan Suplementos Bolivia.
Maintain existing brand integrity: Spartan black & electric yellow palette (#f2e500, #080808, #0D0D0D), official logo, 198 authentic products, and PostgreSQL ACID transational functionality while elevating UI structure with modern Contemporary design patterns (Bento grids, clean card rhythm, micro-typography badges).

## Brand & Aesthetic Fusion
Contemporary UI/UX design focuses on:
- Minimalist, accessible, high-performance architecture
- Bento-grid layouts (asymmetric informational modules, clean focal points)
- High-contrast dark mode surface elevations
- Respecting Spartan Brand: Obsidian Black (#080808), Electric Gold/Yellow (#f2e500), White crisp typography, Emerald transaction accents.

## Style Foundations
- Visual style: Modern, minimal, bold athletic precision
- Typography: Display/Headlines = Anybody & Jost, Body = Hanken Grotesk, Data/SKU/Prices = JetBrains Mono & Overpass Mono
- Spacing scale: Comfortable density mode (8px, 16px, 24px, 32px, 48px)
- Radii: Sharp & disciplined (2px to 6px subtle edges, high-end technical feel)
- Shadows & Borders: 1px high-precision borders (#1A1A1A, #2a2a2a), subtle radial glow accents

## Rules: Don't
